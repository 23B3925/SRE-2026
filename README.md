# Microwave head imaging from 8-antenna S-parameters (DBIM) + IPIGS-style refinement

Reference: *Imaging-Process-Informed Generative Strategy for Microwave Medical Image Processing* (EMBC 2025).

## Pipeline
1. `mmi/sparams.py`   Touchstone `.s8p` reader + audit (reciprocity, passivity).
2. `mmi/phantom.py`   Voxel head (`Head_voxel.dat`) + `Tissue_*GHz.txt` -> eps', sigma at any frequency (PCHIP).
3. `mmi/forward2d.py` 2-D TM Helmholtz FDFD, PML, 4th-order compact scheme (validated vs analytic Green's function).
4. `mmi/scene.py`     Axial slice, 8 antennas on a ring, lesion placement; fine (1.1 mm) / coarse (1.65 mm) grids (no inverse crime).
5. `mmi/dbim.py`      Distorted Born Iterative Method, Gauss-Newton with Gaussian-correlation prior, LM step control,
                      focusing reweighting, joint multi-frequency inversion. **Differential mode** (healthy scan as reference).
6. `mmi/apk.py`       APK classification (paper Eqs. 2-5): HSV hue/value, location, size -> "confused" / "clear".
7. `mmi/metrics.py`   SSIM, IoU, centroid error.   `mmi/render.py` figures.   `mmi/pipeline.py` glue.

## Important limitations (read before the presentation)
* **Your mild/severe `.s8p` files contain no lesion position.** Every off-diagonal S-parameter equals the healthy one times a
  single constant (1.143 mild, 1.322 severe), identical at all frequencies; diagonals unchanged. They cannot be inverted for a
  lesion. Lesion data here are generated by my own forward model at the paper's locations (thalamus ~2 cm disc; ~1 cm arc near skull).
* **Cole-Cole document inconsistency:** its table gives 33.8 / 43.1 / 55.9, not the 36.2 / 52.4 / 64.8 used for the lesion targets.
  I used mild = (52.4, 2.5 S/m), severe = (64.8, 3.5 S/m) at 3.5 GHz.
* **2-D line-source model vs. your 3-D CST patch-antenna helmet.** CST S-parameters cannot be matched quantitatively
  to this model; a calibration step (measured-vs-modelled transfer per port pair) would be required for real data.
* **Anatomy is not measured.** At 3-4 GHz with 8 antennas, whole-head absolute DBIM is not feasible (tissue attenuation ~6 dB/cm;
  absolute inversion gave non-physical images). Images are "healthy phantom + reconstructed contrast". Only the lesion
  comes from data.
* **Dynamic range:** deep lesions need roughly >=100 dB of measurement dynamic range. Best band is 3.0-3.5 GHz.
* Pix2pix: written but not executed (PyTorch could not be installed in the authoring environment).
